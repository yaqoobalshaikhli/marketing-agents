# 01 - What Iraqi consumers can buy today for teleconsultations and family health plans

Date of research: 2026-11-08 (all "seen" dates below are this date unless stated). Researcher: Claude research agent. No TabeebX claims are made in this file.

Evidence types: page_read (I read the page), app_store_page (store listing read), search_snippet (only a search result summary, page not read), aggregator (third-party app directory, not the store itself). Confidence is my confidence that the statement is accurate as written.

## Headline findings

1. The competitor claim (Doctorly, Middle East Hospital, 100,000 IQD per family per year, lab discounts) is NOT FOUND as stated. No page I could reach shows a 100,000 IQD family plan, a family plan of any price, or any link between a "Doctorly" and Middle East Hospital. Confidence that it is not findable with open-web search: 85%. Confidence that it does not exist: unknown (it may be advertised only on Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp, which I was not allowed to open).
2. A product literally named Doctorly (دكتورلي) does exist in Baghdad (doctorlyiq.com plus Play app com.doctorly.app). It has a "Doctorly Gold Card" with "up to 30%" discount on tests and medications, but no price, no family plan, no named partner labs or hospitals. Whether it is the product Yaqoob means: unconfirmed (about 25%). Details in section A.
3. No Iraqi offer I could find sells a subscription or family plan with unlimited teleconsultations. Every Iraqi app with a published price sells per service. Confidence: 80% (coverage gap: WhatsApp-only and Facebook-only sellers are invisible to me).
4. The only published teleconsultation prices in Iraq that I found are Saleem's: online GP 15,000 IQD, nutrition doctor 20,000 IQD, psychiatrist 35,000 IQD per consultation (list prices struck through at 25,000 / 30,000 / 45,000). So 100,000 IQD buys roughly 6 to 7 discounted GP consultations from Saleem. Confidence 90% on the numbers, 70% on what the service includes.
5. "Unlimited" appears on no Iraqi consumer page I read. Where "unlimited" appears at all it is in non-Iraqi Altibbi pages and refers to team users, not consultations.

### A1. Doctorly (دكتورلي), doctorlyiq.com - exists, terms not published
- Source: http://doctorlyiq.com/ plus /services, /about, /contact. page_read, seen 2026-11-08. Confidence 85% on what follows.
- Home page: Arabic site, Baghdad, WhatsApp 07850007505, email info@doctorly.com. It claims video and voice remote consultations, chat with doctors, e-medical file, AI preliminary diagnosis, and "50k+" users. It does not name a company or owner.
- Gold Card: the page offers a unified "بطاقة دكتورلي الذهبية" with e-prescriptions and medical history access, and "exclusive discounts" at "medical centers and pharmacies in its network", "up to 30% on tests and medications" (fetch summary of the page, paraphrased). No price, fee, subscription, family plan, named lab, or redemption method is given. Not found.
- Not on the site: any mention of Middle East Hospital / مستشفى الشرق الأوسط, any IQD amount, any "unlimited" wording (checked home, services, about, contact).
- Signs of an unfinished site (observations, not conclusions): /about shows counters "0+" for patients, doctors, cities and consultations; app-store, privacy and terms links are "#" placeholders; the contact email domain (doctorly.com) differs from the site domain (doctorlyiq.com).
- Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.doctorly.app (app_store_page, parsed from the page HTML, seen 2026-11-08). Name "Doctorly App"; developer ETANA SOFTWARE - FZCO (Dubai Silicon Oasis, UAE) with an Iraqi phone number +964 783 300 0259; released Apr 5, 2026; updated Oct 18, 2026; 1,000+ installs; website field points to doctorlyiq.com, which links this app to the site. This contradicts the site's claim of video/voice consultations. The privacy policy URL on the listing is an IP address (http://72.62.59.111:3001/privacy-policy.html).
- Apple App Store listing for Doctorly: not found.
- Verdict: a similarly named product with a discount card exists. It does not match the claimed 100,000 IQD family plan or the Middle East Hospital tie on anything I can source. I did not assume it is the one Yaqoob means.
