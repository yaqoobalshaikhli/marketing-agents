# TabeebX In-House B2B Corporate Packages

Source: corporate proposal "TabeebX | Baru Medical Ltd" (REF-RML-2026-001, May 2026, prepared for a 160-employee client). These are TabeebX's **own packages, sold directly — no insurance company involved**. Distinct from Health 360 (see `health360.md`), which is the insurance product administered with GlobeMed as TPA. Never mix the two in one quote.

PAYG = pay-as-you-go at corporate discounted rates. All prices IQD, per employee per month. Minimum 10 employees for every package. Companies may mix packages across job grades.

## Positioning per package (from the deck)

- **Economy**: digital-first access for broad workforce coverage.
- **Essential**: growing families needing regular clinical touchpoints and periodic labs.
- **Standard**: full family protection with comprehensive diagnostics and prescription coverage.
- **Premium**: complete peace of mind — specialist access, family-wide, highest limits.

## Service infrastructure (sellable proof points)

- **631 unique lab tests across 18 clinical categories** (cardiac, chemistry, genetics, hematology, hormones, serology, thyroid, PCR, immunity, microbiology, oncology, vitamins…). All lab work requires medical authorization from a TabeebX teleconsultation doctor.
- **Mobile digital X-ray units** that come to the company site or the employee's home; full ultrasound coverage with radiologist digital reports; 12-lead ECG with instant digital report.
- **Nursing in all Baghdad districts**, male and female staff: injections, cannula, wound dressing, drug sensitivity tests, and other specialized services.
- **24/7 online consultations** in internal medicine, general surgery, pediatrics, gynecology, orthopedics.

## Standard contract terms

12-month contract, immediate activation on signing (no waiting period), prices contractually locked for 12 months (zero increases), one monthly invoice based on enrolled headcount (or itemized per department), new employees added at the same rate, package mix across grades allowed, offers valid 60 days from issue. Onboarding commitment in proposal: ~25 employees qualified within 18 business days.

## New model under study (NOT yet launched — internal only)

**Admin-fee / access model**: company pays ~**25,000 IQD per user per YEAR** as an admin fee. Included: teleconsultations only. Everything else (labs, home visits, imaging, physio) is pure PAYG at discounted B2B rates negotiated in the agreement. Logic: near-zero marginal cost to serve, radically lower entry price for cost-sensitive companies, monetization shifts to service volume. Do not include this in customer proposals unless Yaqoob explicitly says the model is approved; it is a pricing experiment.

## Proposal format conventions

Arabic-first deck, confidential footer: "سري و خاص — TabeebX · Baru Medical Ltd — بغداد، العراق — www.tabeebx.com — REF-RML-2026-XXX". Structure: cover (tailored to client name) → client snapshot (headcount, coverage) → integrated offer highlights → 4-package comparison → one page per package → client-specific pricing table → service details → next steps (choose package → sign once → onboarding → immediate coverage) → terms → thank-you.
