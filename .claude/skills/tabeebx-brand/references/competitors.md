# Competitive Landscape (from TabeebX Competitive Brief, April 2026)

12 competitors, 4 tiers. Refresh quarterly — this market moves fast.

## Tier 1 — Direct hybrid competitors (highest threat)

- **ADEED** — home medical services, GCC. Closest model overlap.
- **Manzil Healthcare** — PE-backed, AI-powered (Spectator partnership), actively entering Saudi Arabia. **Most time-sensitive threat**: 12–18 month window to establish position before Manzil consolidates.
- **AIMS Healthcare** — home healthcare, Dubai.
- **First Response Healthcare** — home healthcare, UAE.

## Tier 2 — Telehealth-first (compete for digital health mindshare)

- **Cura** (Saudi), **Altibbi** (MENA-wide, strong Arabic content moat), **Okadoc** (booking-first), **Alma Health** ($10M Series A, chronic care focus).

## Tier 3 — Home healthcare specialists

- **NeoHealth** (UAE launch), **Alleanza Healthcare** (UAE).

## Tier 4 — Platform / government

- **Vezeeta** (booking marketplace), **Sehhaty** (Saudi MOH, 31M users — not a competitor in Iraq but defines Saudi baseline expectations).

## The white space (verified April 2026)

No competitor offers a **comprehensive tiered B2B corporate health package combining telehealth + home visits + lab + physio (+ insurance) as one unified product**. TabeebX's Iraq advantage stacks on top: Iraqi-dialect-native experience, Baghdad provider network via GlobeMed, and almost no direct hybrid competitor operating inside Iraq itself.

## Positioning one-liners

- vs. telehealth-first (Altibbi/Cura): "They stop at the screen. We come to your door — doctor, lab, physio — and insure the whole journey."
- vs. home-care specialists (ADEED/AIMS): "They sell visits. We sell a covered health program your HR can budget per employee per year."
- vs. platforms (Vezeeta): "A directory doesn't take responsibility. A health program does."
