## Coverage limits

- **In-hospital**: IQD 15,000,000 per person per year (annual financial limit; some materials note 3,000,000 sub-limit — verify per plan variant). Geographic scope: Iraq. Network: Private Class A, private room.
- **In-home**: IQD 1,000,000 per person per year; 250,000 IQD per case for specialist home visits.
- **Ambulatory**: IQD 1,000,000 per person per year / 8 visits.
- **Prescribed medicines**: IQD 500,000 per person / 8 visits.
- **Doctor's visit**: IQD 25,000 per visit / 8 visits.
- **Cash-paid claim reimbursement**: 85%.
- Baghdad-scoped variant exists (Geographical Scope: Baghdad) alongside Iraq-wide.

## In-hospital benefits (selected)

Covered: medical/surgical/endoscopic treatment, emergency treatment, appendectomy, pre-operative tests, physiotherapy related to covered hospitalization, parental accommodation, morgue & burial up to IQD 500,000, uninterrupted hospitalization up to 15 days after policy expiry.

Not covered: home care following hospitalization, hospital daily indemnity.

## Maternity & congenital

Covered: normal & cesarean delivery (cesarean on medical request), newborn from day 14, free insurance for eligible newborns, nursery boarding, incubator up to 3 days, one pediatric consultation per delivery, circumcision during first stay, epidural, 25 congenital cases correctable by surgery, maternity complications incl. medically justified abortion, Guthrie test.

Not covered: other congenital cases, amniocentesis, non-medically-mandated abortion.

## GlobeMed relationship (provider agreement, dated 12/7/2026)

- GlobeMed Iraq (Overseas Administrative Services Co., reg. Baghdad 02-0000468, 2016) is a neutral third-party administrator between providers and insurers.
- TabeebX operates as a licensed healthcare provider in the network: eligibility verification, coverage decisions issued by GlobeMed, coded claims, medical-file access for audit, exclusive handling of GlobeMed clients through GlobeMed.
- Contract services scope: hospitalization, day-case procedures, emergency, outpatient diagnostics, outpatient treatments, medicines, consultations, dental.
