# Product marketing context: TabeebX

The vendored marketing skills (`.claude/skills/social`, `copywriting`, `ads` and the rest) read this file first. It is a short summary. The full, authoritative context is the `tabeebx-brand` skill (`.claude/skills/tabeebx-brand/`). Where the two differ, that skill wins.

- **Company:** TabeebX (legal entity Baru Medical Ltd), Baghdad, Iraq. Digital health: home doctor, nurse and lab visits (631 tests), mobile X-ray, ultrasound, ECG and physiotherapy at home; teleconsultation (app in development); in-house B2B corporate health packages; Health 360 family insurance with GlobeMed as TPA (separate product, never mixed with the packages).
- **Current marketing focus (from 2026-10-08):** B2C home-service bookings and brand awareness in Baghdad. Bookings arrive on WhatsApp. B2B and Health 360 come later.
- **Audience:** Baghdad families and young professionals tired of clinic queues; adult children caring for elderly parents.
- **Positioning:** «صحتك مو رفاهية، صحتك أولوية». We sell peace of mind (راحة بال), not medicine. Trust campaign «لا تصدگنا. شوف بعينك.»: show, don't claim.
- **Voice:** Baghdadi Iraqi colloquial for consumers (the `iraqi-dialect` skill), MSA or English for B2B. Warm, direct, respectful of doctors.
- **Channels:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp.
- **Constraints:**
  - Paid budget under $500 a month; organic testing first.
  - Solo founder approves everything.
  - Claude Pro plan.
  - Canva Free.
  - No Higgsfield credits.
- **Differentiator:** the only unified telehealth + home care + insurance offer in Iraq, in Iraqi Arabic, with a real provider network.
- **Competitors in view:**
  - Baghdad home labs advertising home blood draws (see `data/competitors/watchlist.csv`)
  - MENA telehealth (Altibbi, Vezeeta, Okadoc)
  - GCC home care (ADEED, Manzil, AIMS)
- **Hard rules:**
  - No fabricated testimonials.
  - AI people are labeled.
  - No implied health conditions.
  - No cure claims.
  - No prices or «تأمين» in consumer copy.
  - Nothing publishes and no money is spent without Yaqoob's approval.
