Verdict: **REVISE**

Top 3 fixes:
1. **Redraw the lit door on slides 5 and 6 so it reads as an open door, not a numbered slab.** As rendered (x 329–497, y 888–1120), the solid blue round-topped fill with «٣» set into it, on a ground line, reads as a headstone at phone size and in greyscale. It sits on the payoff slide of a health piece. Add one door-specific cue (for example an open door leaf hinged on a jamb, with the blue as the light in the opening), and move «٣» out of the door shape. Use the same drawing as piece 02's frame-2 door (its round-1 fix 1) so the week's doorway stays one symbol. Raises Glaser, Rand, Bass and Cooper.
2. **Stop the hierarchy relying on colour alone.** In greyscale, every pink pivot drops to mid-grey and becomes the weakest line on its slide. On all seven slides, set the white setup words in Cairo Medium and keep only the pink group in Cairo Bold, as in piece 02's fix 2, so the system stays one. On slide 7, also give the CTA «رابط الواتساب بالبايو.» a clear size step over the question: it is currently 88 px, the same as the question. Re-check every line against the 936 px measure without re-breaking the copy. Raises Scher, Spiekermann and Glaser.
3. **Get two Copywriter confirmations and apply them.** (a) A line break in slide 2's «ونطرة ما تعرف شگد تطول.», so the slide sets at the house 88 px instead of a one-off 76 px. (b) Slide 7's order: the copy runs licence line, then question, then CTA (proof before CTA, as in the brief). The render moves the licence line under the CTA, and the Design notes don't mention it. Restore the copy's order or get it approved. In the same pass, snap the logo slot to the column grid (x 72–368).

Not blocking this review: the official logo file and its reversed lockup or white plate (Yaqoob); the cover hook's line break (Copywriter, open item 1); the at-posting wa.me link for the Facebook caption. The critic did not edit the piece file or any design file.
