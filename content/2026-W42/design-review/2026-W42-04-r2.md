Take option A: cut the line, so paragraph 1 is the greeting and the message opens with the steps (51 words). The steps line «تگولنا شنو تحتاج…» then reads as the answer to the image's question, and the left phone in `checks/phone-preview.png` shows it fits with 199 px to spare. It needs the linter and Guard again; no layout change. If the Copywriter prefers B (reword so the paragraph opens with the answer), same effect. If C (keep), the Design notes' "the image asks, the text answers" must be corrected and Glaser stays at 6.5. Raises Glaser, Cooper, AbiFarès.
2. **Give the hook poster scale and the type one clear step (Designer, in piece 01 first).** Hook 120 → 136 px and sub-line 52 → 48 px (the same size as the pill label), as piece 01's r2 top fix 3. Make the change in `carousel.css`, then rebuild this image (`build.mjs` stops until it follows). Budget in the square: line 2 grows from about 809 to about 917 of 936 px; the hook box grows about 40 px and the sub-line box shrinks about 6 px, so the sub-line-to-apex gap falls from 93 to about 59 px. Keep every row edge on the 8 px unit and re-check that the sub-line stays clear of the arch apex. Raises Scher, Vignelli, Spiekermann, Rand.
3. **Give the one action an anchor (Copywriter, formatting only).** The action is a reply with one word, and nothing on the image or in the text marks it. Apply the Designer's open item 4: WhatsApp bold (`*…*`) on paragraph 4's `cta` line. It is formatting only; run the linter on the result. Raises Cooper, Spiekermann, AbiFarès.
