Verdict: **REVISE** (hard gates pass and no lens is below 6, but the average is under 8.0). Round 2 of 3; round 3 is the last before ESCALATE.

Top 3 fixes, in priority order:
1. **Make the arrival at the door ownable (lifts Glaser and Bass).** At 9.1 to 9.4 s a generic map pin is the payoff of the piece's only twist. Replace it with an arrival image only TabeebX could show in the lit doorway (derived from the raised-arms gesture, never the altered mark), so the door idea completes on a symbol of care arriving. This is the single biggest gap: the best-made beat in the video ends on the least specific image.
2. **Sign the video from the first frame and give the mark scale (lifts Rand).** The cover frame and the loop's first frame carry no TabeebX sign, and the logo is 151 px of a 300 px cap. Show the official logo from 0.0 s at about 220 px, at one fixed place, after checking it clears the F7 key.
3. **Make the pink type the largest mass on every card it can (lifts Scher).** The solid blue open door outweighs the key from 9.1 s and the one-line keys are small in the frame. Set F3 «الموعد.» and a stacked F4 «لحد / البيت.» at about 200 px, keeping two sizes per frame.

Also needed to reach 8.0, because the margin is thin and these stack: (4) retime F6, F4, F2 and F5 so no hold sits on its minimum (Spiekermann, Chahine); (5) hold F6's picture zone on one row with one bubble (Vignelli, and it takes F6 from 9 elements to 8); (6) make the F4 and F5 key moves readable at about 160 px of travel (Cooper); (7) one Baghdad-specific detail on the door (AbiFarès); (8) Copywriter confirms the four line breaks (Boutros).

Keep in the revision: the one door across 01 to 03, the declared rows and right axis, the three-beat turn after the line settles, F6 as the longest card, the hook fully on screen at 0.0 s, whole-word right-to-left motion, the clean Arabic, the unaltered logo file, and the 20.0 s length.

For others, outside this lens set: the Copywriter should confirm the four line breaks and update the copy-check table to the new F4 to F7 times. Guard should check, as the Designer asked, that the F6 typing balloon doesn't imply how a doctor explains results, and that the map pin (or its replacement) says "at your door" and nothing about who comes.
