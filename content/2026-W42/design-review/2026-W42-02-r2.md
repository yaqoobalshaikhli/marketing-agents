Verdict: **REVISE**

Top 3 fixes, in priority order:
1. **Give the Question sticker a clean landing on frame 1 (and the link sticker a bigger one on frame 2).** As specified, the 480 px card is the same width as the door's interior (4 px each side) and its top corners cross the leaf arch and the frame's edge. Define a zone wholly in the straight part of the door with at least 24 px clear, move the knob out of the way, and show it in the guide with the Copywriter's Arabic prompt at real size. On frame 2, widen the lit opening so the link sticker can sit near 45% of the screen width. This raises Glaser, Vignelli, Rand, Spiekermann and Chahine.
2. **Let the type lead the door.** The key words lead by only 1.44×, and on frame 2 the blue lit area has about 6× the ink of all the type. Put «للبيت؟» on its own line at about 144 px on frame 1, and make frame 2's light a narrower band so the pink line is the heaviest thing on the frame. This raises Scher and Bass.
3. **Settle the hold time.** Confirm on a device how long a photo story stays up. If it is about 5 s, the Copywriter cuts frame 1 to what can be read in that time (about 10 words by the house rule, against 18 now). This raises Cooper and Chahine.

Smaller, to do in the same pass: make the sub row one weight (Medium) on both frames; keep the inset leaf line on the open leaf.

Not blocking, for others: the "Needs Yaqoob" line and the hand-off in the piece file still say the logo file is needed (decision #66 supersedes it); Guard must re-run because the copy changed after its 17 Oct PASS. This is round 2 of 3: if round 3 does not pass, the piece is marked ESCALATE.

What improved since round 1 (6.75): gate 7 now passes (official logo, unaltered, one place on both frames); the door reads as a door and visibly opens; the key words lead by size; the privacy line is read before the sticker. The remaining gap to 8 is the sticker landing, how little the type leads, and the unresolved word count and hold time.

The critic did not edit the piece file or any design file.
