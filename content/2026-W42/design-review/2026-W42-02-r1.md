Verdict: **REVISE**

Top 3 fixes:
1. **Gate 7 (blocking): sign it with the official logo.** On both frames, replace the placeholder with `design-studio/assets/brand/tabeebx-logo-stacked-on-navy.png`, unaltered. Use one size and one position on the column grid. Re-plan the 304×112 horizontal slot for the roughly square stacked lockup (at most ~300 px wide), with the wordmark legible at phone size and the Arabic still leading the header. This raises Rand, Vignelli and Boutros.
2. **Make the doorway a door, and make it open.** Use piece 01's proportions (taller than wide) plus one door cue, so frame 1 says "your front door" at thumbnail size. On frame 2, make the change read as the door opening, not a flat blue slab that reads as a closed panel or a headstone. This raises Glaser, Bass, Rand and Cooper.
3. **Let the key words lead by size or weight, not colour alone.** Give «للبيت؟» (frame 1) and «دزلنا رسالة واتساب.» (frame 2) a step over the white setup words, so each frame's hierarchy holds in greyscale and the action on frame 2 is read first. This raises Scher and Spiekermann.

Also before Guard (not in the top 3, but needed for a pass):
- Move frame 1's privacy line «لا تكتب اسمك ولا شي عن صحتك.» so it is read before the sticker (AbiFarès). Guard should check this too, because the line protects health details.
- The Copywriter needs to supply the Question sticker's prompt (Chahine).
- Confirm each frame's on-screen hold at posting (Cooper).
