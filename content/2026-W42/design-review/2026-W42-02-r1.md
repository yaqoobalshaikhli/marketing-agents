Verdict: **REVISE**

Top 3 fixes:
1. **Make frame 2's doorway read as opened, not filled.** The flat blue arch has no door cue, and at small size it can read as an arched window or a headstone-like slab. Add one door-specific cue, such as an open door leaf (navy panel with the same 12 px blue line) hinged on a jamb. Keep it clear of the link-sticker zone (x 270–810, y 1164–1276) and inside the 72 px margins. Raises Glaser, Rand, Bass and Cooper.
2. **Stop the hierarchy relying on colour alone.** On both frames, set the white setup words in Cairo Medium and keep only the pink group («للبيت؟» / «دزلنا رسالة واتساب.») in Cairo Bold. Medium sets narrower, so re-fit the headline to the largest size where each copy line still fits 936 px, without re-breaking the copy. Then re-check the «شي» dot clearance. The pivot then survives the greyscale test and gains weight contrast against the setup. Raises Scher, Glaser and Vignelli.
3. **Settle the Question sticker's prompt line before round 2.** Get it from the Copywriter, or get their confirmation that it repeats «سؤال», and render it in the `frame-01-guide` mock. As posted, it is the most central text on frame 1 and is currently unverified (the default could show in the app's language). Also snap the logo slot to the column grid (x 72–368) while in the file.

Not blocking this review: the reversed logo lockup or white plate (Yaqoob), and the at-posting wa.me link. The critic did not edit the piece file or any design file.
