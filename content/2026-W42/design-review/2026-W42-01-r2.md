Top 3 fixes (priority order):
1. **Make the arrival the biggest object in the piece.** Slide 5's open door is 168 px wide (x 329–497, y 888–1120) under the densest text, and the other step slides leave y 600–880 empty. Let the lit station grow about its ground point, starting with the door on slide 5 at about 1.4× (x ~296–530, apex ~y 795). It clears the text: the list ends at y 725, the punch line «بلا سرة ولا طريق.» is at x ≥ 640, and the door stays 100+ px from any pink. The stations keep their x positions and the ground line stays at y 1120. Raises Glaser, Scher, Bass, Cooper and Rand.
2. **Fix slide 7's tiers and clear space.** Licence line 48 → 42 px (labels may go to 40), so the steps read 42 / 60 / 96; add about 24 px of clear space between the logo's wordmark and the licence line, taken from the question's leading (1.4 → 1.3), keeping the copy's order and the 96 px CTA, and re-check that the CTA stays at least 70 px from the band's blue. Raises Spiekermann, Rand and Vignelli.
3. **Give the cover's hook poster scale.** Hook 120 → 136 px (line 2 grows from 813 to about 921 px of the 936 px measure) and the sub-line 52 → 48 px (the house label size), for about 2.8:1, and move the sub-line down to clear the taller hook.
