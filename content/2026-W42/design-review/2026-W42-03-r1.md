Verdict: **REVISE** (hard gate 7 fails, and the average is below 8.0; Rand is below 6)

Top 3 fixes:
1. **Logo (gate 7, and gate 5 on the placeholder text).** Replace the dashed "[LOGO: official file needed]" slot (4.9 s to the end) with `design-studio/assets/brand/tabeebx-logo-stacked-on-navy.png`, unaltered. The stacked lockup is square, so give it a header zone about 150 px tall (at most 300 px wide) instead of the 80 px slot, and re-run the safe-zone and collision checks. Keep it on the end card where the piece resolves.
2. **Make the turn land.** At «ونجيك لحد البيت.» (7.7–10.1 s) the door opens onto an empty, flat-blue arch, and the swing overlaps the line's entrance. Give the swing its own beat after the line settles, make the opened door read as an opening (leaf edge or depth), and let something visibly arrive at the threshold. Then retime F6 «ودكتور يشرحها إلك.» to the longest still hold of the six cards, using the 0.8 s spare under 20 s. This lifts Glaser, Bass and Cooper.
3. **Type as the strong element.** The pink key is 104 px against a 64 px lead (1.63:1) in a band about 16 % of the frame tall. Make the key phrase at least about 2.5× the lead, breaking longer keys onto two lines, so the words, not the pictogram, carry each card. This lifts Scher, AbiFarès and Rand's thumbnail test.

Keep in the revision: the declared rows and the fixed landing spot for pink, the one motion vocabulary, the hook fully on screen at 0.0 s, the right-to-left entrances and exits, the clean Arabic, and the door as the shared W42 symbol.

Minor, no score effect alone: the stray calendar dot at 4.9–5.06 s (dasharray shorter than the tick path).

For others, outside this lens set: the Copywriter should confirm the F2–F6 line breaks. Guard should check, as the Designer asked, that the F6 typing balloon doesn't imply how a doctor explains results.
