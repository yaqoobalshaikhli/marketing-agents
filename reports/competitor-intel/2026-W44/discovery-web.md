## Method and limits

- **Searches:** 31 `WebSearch` calls in standard mode (a little over the 20 to 30 guide, because Iraq results were thin). Several used the allowed-domain filter for play.google.com and apps.apple.com, which gave the best results. The search tool is US-only.
- **Fetches:** 4 `WebFetch` calls, all failed with `ENOTFOUND` (3 for play.google.com, 1 for mwm.ai), sent in parallel. Per the tool message I stopped fetching. If Yaqoob wants store pages read (update dates, download counts, developer details), play.google.com and mwm.ai need to be allowed in the session's network settings. apps.apple.com and other hosts were not tried.
- **Evidence quality:** every row rests on a search-tool summary of a listing, not on the page itself. Feature lists are the apps' own marketing text. For that reason no confidence is above 60%.
- **Compliance:** no facebook.com, instagram.com or tiktok.com page was opened; Perplexity and scraper services were not used; no one was contacted; no personal data about private individuals was collected (the only named people in sources are company founders in public funding news, and I recorded none).
